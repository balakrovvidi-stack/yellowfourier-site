The program

Three phases, one manuscript.

A structured 6–9 month arc that takes a student from foundations to a submitted research paper — mentored the whole way by senior research scientists.

The journey

How the cohort unfolds.

01
Months 1–3

Foundations

Core concepts of the field, research methods, reading the literature, and the computational and statistical toolkit every researcher needs.

02
Months 4–6

The problem

Hands-on work on an assigned problem statement — designing experiments, generating and analyzing data, and building models toward a result.

03
Months 6–9

The manuscript

Coding toward the finding, writing the paper, and preparing a formal research submission worthy of an international audience.

Weekly online cohort discussionsLive sessions with mentors and fellow researchers, every week.
Required student commitment · ~10 hrs/weekA real, expected commitment — research this serious can't be done in an afternoon.
Fields of study

Where the work happens.

Computational Neuroscience

Machine learning and signal processing on brain-imaging data — the lab's anchor domain and deepest bench of projects.

Biomedical Engineering

Turning biological questions into computational problems — modeling, measurement, and interpretable analysis of physiological data.

Electrical & Power Systems

Signals, systems, and data-driven methods applied to the grid and modern electrical engineering challenges.
