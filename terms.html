Terms & Enrollment Agreement.

The program, tuition, payment terms, and policies that govern enrollment in a Yellow Fourier Research Labs cohort. Please read these before enrolling; completing an enrollment payment confirms your acceptance of them.

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies to the cohort beginning September 20, 2026.

1. Who we are

Yellow Fourier Research Labs ("Yellow Fourier," "we," "us") is an independent, mentored research cohort program operated by Nanogen Solutions LLC (California, USA). Yellow Fourier is an independent organization and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university, institute, or company. Any reference to an institution describes an individual mentor's background only.

Contact: admissions@yellowfourier.ai · +1 650 430 8359 · yellowfourier.ai

2. The program and what enrollment covers

Enrollment reserves one student's place in the cohort, which is capped at approximately five students and runs six to nine months under senior research scientists. The program runs in three phases:

  • Foundations (months 1–3) — core concepts, research methods, reading the literature, and the computational and statistical toolkit.
  • The Problem (months 4–6) — the student is assigned a well-scoped research problem, designs experiments, generates and analyzes data, and builds models.
  • The Manuscript (months 6–9) — coding toward the finding, writing the paper, and preparing a formal research submission.

Fields of study are Computational Neuroscience, Biomedical Engineering, and Electrical & Power Systems. Enrollment includes weekly online cohort discussions, direct mentorship from the research faculty, the assigned research problem and data, and full support through manuscript preparation. Expected student commitment is approximately 10 hours per week.

3. What is guaranteed — and what is not

Guaranteed: a real, citable research output. Every student who completes the cohort finishes with a manuscript submitted to a public preprint archive (such as arXiv), authored by the student.

Not promised: acceptance at any peer-reviewed conference or journal. That outcome is competitive, is determined solely by independent review, and is never guaranteed.

4. Admissions and enrollment

Admission is by application and interview, not by open sale. Families register through this website, attend an orientation with the researchers, and — if a place is offered — are informed of the program, its schedule, and the fee before any payment is taken. Enrollment is confirmed only after the enrollment fee has been paid for the offered student. Seats are limited and are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis among invited families.

5. Tuition and fees

The enrollment fee (tuition) is $5,000 USD per student, covering one student's place in one cohort and everything described in Section 2. The fee is a fixed, one-time tuition per cohort seat; there are no recurring charges, and Yellow Fourier does not enroll students in any subscription or automatic renewal. Families are invoiced and informed of the amount before the card is charged.

6. Payment terms

  • Payments are processed securely through Authorize.Net (a Visa solution). Card details are entered only on Authorize.Net's secure hosted payment page and are never stored by Yellow Fourier.
  • The charge appears on card statements with the descriptor "YELLOW FOURIER."
  • Each charge is made only with the cardholder's knowledge and consent, after the family has been informed of the program, schedule, and fee.
  • Yellow Fourier will never ask you for a full card number, expiry date, or CVV by email, text, or WhatsApp. Please never send full card details through those channels.
  • Bank transfer (ACH) is available on request as an alternative to card payment — contact admissions.

7. Refunds and cancellations

Because each cohort is capped at roughly five seats and the enrollment fee reserves a specific place, the fee is charged only after a family has been informed of the program, schedule, and fee, and has confirmed enrollment. Once enrollment is confirmed and the seat is reserved, the enrollment fee is non-refundable, except where required by applicable law. If your circumstances change, please contact admissions as early as possible — we will review reasonable, exceptional requests in good faith. Families who enroll confirm that the charge was authorized with their knowledge and consent and agree not to initiate a chargeback for a charge they have authorized; any billing question should be raised with us first at admissions@yellowfourier.ai so we can resolve it directly.

8. Privacy

We collect only the information families provide to register and enroll — parent/guardian and student names, contact details, and program interests — and we use it solely to run admissions and the cohort. We do not sell personal information or use paid lead generation. Payment card information is handled by Authorize.Net under its own security and PCI standards; we do not receive or store full card numbers. To ask what we hold or request deletion, email admissions@yellowfourier.ai.

9. Independence & accuracy

Yellow Fourier Research Labs is an independent organization and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any institution with which its principals are or were associated; institutional references describe individual background only. We aim to keep this page and the site accurate; if anything here differs from what you were told during admissions, contact us and we will clarify.

Questions about these terms, tuition, or payment? Email admissions@yellowfourier.ai or call/text +1 650 430 8359. Merchant of record: Nanogen Solutions LLC (Authorize.Net: YELLOW FOURIER).
