What this is

Not a summer camp. An apprenticeship on a live research agenda.

Most research programs for young students stop at exposure. We start students inside a real, active research program — the way research careers actually begin. Each student is assigned a well-scoped problem, learns the methods to attack it, produces and analyzes real data, and writes it up for submission. They learn to think like a researcher years before college.

Outcome

An honest promise.

What we guarantee

A real, citable research output.

Every student who completes the cohort finishes with a written manuscript submitted to a public preprint archive (such as arXiv) — a genuine piece of research, authored by them, that exists in the world and can be cited.

What we don't

We never promise acceptance.

Publication at peer-reviewed international conferences is competitive and never certain — no honest program can guarantee it. We aim high and prepare students for that bar, but the guaranteed floor is the archive submission above.

Seats are limited to about five per cohort.
